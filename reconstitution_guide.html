Universal Reconstitution

The complete laboratory protocol for reconstituting lyophilized peptides, including standard math and syringe conversions.

The Fundamentals

Every research peptide arrives as a stable, dry powder. Before it can be utilized in any protocol, it must be dissolved into a liquid state. The process of adding a diluent to a lyophilized powder is called reconstitution.

Lyophilization

❄️ Freeze-dried preservation
🛡️ Protects fragile bonds
📦 Survives room-temp transit

Bacteriostatic Water

🧪 Contains 0.9% Benzyl Alcohol
🛑 Inhibits bacterial growth
📅 Allows for multi-use vials

U-100 Syringes

💉 Standard insulin syringe
📏 100 units = 1.0mL total
🔍 Ensures micro-dosing precision

Step-by-Step Protocol

Sterility and gentle handling are paramount. Follow these exact steps to ensure you do not damage the molecular structure of the peptide.

1

Prep & Sanitize 🧼

Wash hands thoroughly. Pop the plastic caps off both the Peptide vial and the BAC water vial. Swab both rubber stoppers vigorously with an alcohol prep pad and let them air dry completely.

2

Draw BAC Water 💉

Using a sterile mixing syringe (often larger than an insulin syringe, e.g., a 3mL luer-lock), draw the mathematically required amount of Bacteriostatic Water. Ensure there are no large air bubbles in the syringe.

3

Inject Slowly 💧

Pierce the peptide vial. Do not spray the water directly onto the powder. Angle the needle so the water drips slowly down the glass wall of the vial. Direct pressure can destroy fragile peptide bonds.

4

Swirl to Dissolve 🔄

NEVER SHAKE. Shaking will fracture the peptide chains. Instead, gently roll the vial between your palms or swirl it slowly in circles until the solution is completely clear.

Peptide Math & Conversions

The Golden Formula

Peptide math confuses many researchers, but it is simple division: (Total Milligrams in Vial × 1000) ÷ Milliliters of BAC added = Micrograms (mcg) per 1.0mL. Divide that number by 100 to find out exactly how much peptide is in every 1 unit on your insulin syringe.

Standard insulin syringes are measured in Units (U-100). Therefore, 10 Units = 0.1mL. Below are the most common reconstitution ratios.

Peptide Vial Size BAC Water Added Concentration Yield Dose per 10 Units (0.1mL)
5mg 2.0 mL 2.5 mg per mL 250 mcg
10mg 2.0 mL 5.0 mg per mL 500 mcg
10mg 1.0 mL 10.0 mg per mL 1,000 mcg (1mg)
30mg 3.0 mL 10.0 mg per mL 1,000 mcg (1mg)

Storage & Handling

Temperature

Prior to mixing, lyophilized powder can remain at room temperature. Once reconstituted with BAC, it must be refrigerated (36°F to 46°F).

Light Exposure

Peptides degrade quickly when exposed to UV light. Store your reconstituted vials in a dark container or the back of the refrigerator.

Shelf Life

The benzyl alcohol in BAC water preserves sterility for roughly 28 days after mixing. Vials older than a month should be safely discarded.
