Tuscarora Arboretum

A local-history and archaeology project from Martinsburg, West Virginia — named for Tuscarora Creek, along which the town was laid out in the 1770s. Objects recovered from historic ground are researched exhaustively — patent records, glasshouse catalogs, local history — and published here as full guides with audio briefings.

The Collection

Artifacts

Objects dug from the earth at historic Martinsburg sites — glass bottles, marbles, china, and more. Each artifact is photographed, researched to primary sources, and published with a complete guide and an audio brief.
