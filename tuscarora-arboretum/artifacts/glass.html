Glass

Glass is the great survivor of the historic backyard — chemically near-immortal in the ground, it outlasts the labels, the houses, and sometimes the streets themselves. Three families of glass finds are tracked here.

Glass Artifacts

Bottles

Drugstore, toiletry, and household bottles from Martinsburg ground. First entry: Artifact No. 1, the Queen Street Bottle — a ribbed flat-oval flask dug at 117 South Queen Street, researched through its patent marking, its glass, and its ground.

View Bottles

Marbles

Handmade and machine-made glass marbles — clay-era transitions, German swirls, and early machine marbles turn up wherever children played and privies were filled. The first marble entries are being prepared.
