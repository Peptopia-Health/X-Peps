Harvest by maturity

Leaf is picked by maturity stage for the target vein — early for whites, mid-cycle for greens, fully mature for reds. Vein color as a harvest decision, not a dye job.

Dry under cover

Indoor/under-cover drying protects the leaf from UV degradation and rain contamination during the cure — the unglamorous step that separates clean leaf from musty leaf.

Devein & sort

Stems and veins removed, leaf sorted by grade. The "Super" tier strains are the larger, fully-developed leaves from this sort.

Mill fine

Micro-milled to a fine, consistent powder. Grind consistency matters more than most buyers realize — uneven grinds brew unevenly.

Test the batch

Every lot goes to the independent ISO 17025 lab for the 3-panel protocol: alkaloids (HPLC-UV + LC-MS/MS), heavy metals (ICP-MS), microbials.

Pack & seal

Hand-packed in New York and heat-sealed. Small-batch packing keeps lots distinct instead of blending harvests into anonymity.

Label with batch

Each pouch carries its batch number and a QR code linking to that batch's COA — the traceability loop closed at the consumer's end.

Whole leaf only — no extracts, no 7-OH products. In a market flooded with enhanced products, a vendor that refuses to sell them is making a deliberate integrity choice, and the lab data backs it up.
