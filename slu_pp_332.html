SLU-PP-332: The Exercise Mimetic

An experimental small molecule that replicates the cellular and metabolic benefits of endurance exercise—increasing fat oxidation and stamina without central nervous system stimulation or appetite suppression.

A Breakthrough in Exercise Mimicry

Cellular Energy & Endurance Without the Treadmill

SLU-PP-332 is an innovative experimental compound engineered to simulate the profound metabolic adaptations traditionally triggered by sustained cardiovascular training.

Pioneering researchers at the University of Florida and collaborating academic institutions have demonstrated that this small molecule targets fundamental cellular pathways responsible for energy expenditure, fat burning, and muscle fiber transformation.

Crucially, SLU-PP-332 operates without suppressing caloric intake or requiring additional physical output, opening new avenues for researching metabolic health and muscle preservation.

Key Compound Summary

  • Primary Target Estrogen-Related Receptor Alpha (ERRα) Agonist
  • Metabolic Mode Enhances resting fatty acid oxidation & energy expenditure
  • CNS Impact Non-stimulant mechanism (no elevated heart rate or jitters)
  • Research Status Preclinical lab & animal model phase

How SLU-PP-332 Works

Activating genetic programs that simulate prolonged endurance workouts.

ERRα Receptor Activation

SLU-PP-332 acts as a potent agonist (activator) for Estrogen-Related Receptors (ERRs), with a specific affinity for ERRα. These nuclear receptors regulate key metabolic gene networks in energy-demanding tissues such as skeletal muscle, liver, and heart.

Transcriptional Reprogramming

Upon activation, ERRα triggers gene transcription programs identical to those naturally activated during prolonged aerobic cardio. This stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis and shifts cellular energy production toward lipid utilization.

Key Potential Benefits (Animal Studies)

Preclinical findings from rodent models demonstrate widespread metabolic and physical enhancements.

Boosts Fat Metabolism

Encourages muscle and hepatic tissue to preferentially burn fatty acids for cellular energy rather than storing them in adipose tissue.

Aids Weight Loss

Reduces total fat mass and body weight in obese test subjects without requiring reduced food consumption or forced physical exertion.

Improves Blood Sugar

Enhances peripheral insulin sensitivity and glucose clearance, supporting healthy metabolic homeostasis in insulin-resistant subjects.

Protects the Liver

Lowers lipid accumulation inside liver cells, offering protective effects against hepatic steatosis and fatty liver progression.

Increases Stamina

Significantly improves physical endurance capacity, enabling mice to run longer distances and withstand sustained exertion.

Supports Muscle Health

Promotes fatigue-resistant oxidative muscle fibers (Type I) and helps counteract muscle wasting caused by age or inactivity.

Non-Stimulant Mechanism

Unlike conventional fat burners or metabolic agents, SLU-PP-332 operates exclusively via nuclear receptor gene transcription rather than central nervous system (CNS) stimulation. As a result, it does not induce tachycardia, elevated blood pressure, anxiety, or jitters.

Current Research Status

Important regulatory and experimental context

Preclinical Phase

All current data and reported benefits stem from laboratory cellular assays and rodent (mouse) model studies. Long-term human pharmacokinetics remain under investigation.

Not Approved for Human Use

SLU-PP-332 is an investigational research chemical and is not approved by regulatory authorities (such as the FDA) for clinical treatment, human consumption, or diagnostic procedures.
