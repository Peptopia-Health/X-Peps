Mini Intake

One page, start to finish — the arrival tasks, then the five CPRS notes in the order they run.

Off tours: rescue meds PO & IM in place before the patient enters the unit
First — Before & On Arrival
  • Before: consent signed · admission orders + rescue meds in CPRS · CSRE done · COVID negative
  • Check: ED vitals & labs · BAL ≤ 299 for alcohol admissions · equipment needs (CPAP, walker…)
  • ED calls: get report, evaluate the Veteran, IV out, ED staff escort up
  • Vitals + height & weight · issue BP cuff labeled name + room
  • Hygiene bag (+ Oral Care) · 6A folder · Rules of Behavior · Behavioral Agreement signed → unit clerk
  • 2 staff skin check + hazardous-items search (under underwear & socks) · clothes washed · white bag + valuables envelope up · extras to baggage · not on roster → call AOD

Note 5

VAAES Skin Inspection/Assessment

from the 2-staff inspection · cosign
  • Inspection: skin color · temperature · moisture · turgor — completed with a second RN
  • Braden: Sensory · Moisture · Activity · Mobility · Nutrition · Friction/Shear → total + risk statement · Interventions: needed / not needed · Skin integrity
  • Close with: hazardous items found / none found during the skin assessment
Finish
  • Mental Health Suite — Care Plan: New Plan – Profile · 1 problem · 1 goal (their words) · 1 objective · 4+ interventions, daily
  • Blurb: synopsis in Word → print → tape on NOK
  • Verify orders + VISTA Kardex E, E (DX · allergies · diet · advance directive · flags · CIWA/COWS · finger sticks · 1:1)
  • Reminders: COVID reminder complete · PHQ-9 / Columbia done if due
