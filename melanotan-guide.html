They share a name and a parent hormone — and almost nothing else. Melanotan I is the gentle, skin-only original. Melanotan II is the stronger, wider-acting ring that also stirs up libido, appetite, and nausea. Both reach users the same way: a vial, mixed with water, injected under the skin.

In the 1980s, researchers at the University of Arizona built longer-lasting copies of α-MSH — the body's own tanning signal — hoping a drug-made tan might protect fair skin. Melanotan I came first (1980): the full hormone, toughened with two swaps. Melanotan II followed in 1989: just the active core, closed into a ring.

Melanotan I went on to become a licensed medicine for a rare light-sensitivity disease. Melanotan II was never licensed anywhere — the internet adopted it instead.

Both carry the same four-letter core — His-Phe-Arg-Trp — that every melanocortin receptor recognises. Melanotan I wraps it in the full hormone, so it mostly speaks to the skin. Melanotan II strips everything else away and locks the core in a ring: smaller, tougher, and heard by almost every receptor in the family.

Five receptors, five jobs. A compound's personality is just the list of receptors it can reach:

Melanotan I works the pigment row and leaves the rest alone — a tan, and little else. Melanotan II works four rows at once, including the brain rows behind erections, appetite loss, nausea, and blood pressure. That one difference explains everything else in this guide.

Both compounds arrive as freeze-dried powder in a 10 mg vial. Here is the whole routine, start to finish.

Reconstitution Basics

Lyophilized (freeze-dried) melanotan must be reconstituted with Bacteriostatic Water (BAC) before use. Follow these standard sterile handling protocols:

1

Prep & Sanitize 🧼

Wash hands thoroughly. Pop the plastic caps off both the melanotan vial and the BAC water vial. Swab both rubber stoppers with an alcohol prep pad and let air dry.

2

Draw BAC Water 💉

Using a sterile mixing syringe, draw 2 mL of Bacteriostatic Water. (Adding 2 mL to a 10 mg vial yields a concentration of 1 mg per 0.2 mL / 20 units.)

3

Inject & Dissolve 💧

Pierce the peptide vial. Do not spray directly onto the powder. Angle the needle so the water drips slowly down the glass wall of the vial to protect the fragile peptide bonds.

4

Swirl Gently 🔄

NEVER SHAKE. Gently roll or swirl the vial between your fingers until the solution is completely clear and dissolved. It is now ready for use — and goes straight into the refrigerator.

The injection itself is subcutaneous: pinched skin of the abdomen or thigh, sites rotated, a fresh sterile needle every time. Evening dosing is the common pattern for Melanotan II — nausea and flushing, when they come, arrive in the first couple of hours.

Standard Dosing Schedule — Melanotan II

To build colour gradually and keep side effects down 🤢, users follow a step-up schedule, injecting once daily while loading:

This is the community-standard schedule, not a medical one — no melanotan injection schedule is approved anywhere, and the only dose ever run in controlled trials was 0.025 mg/kg/day. Vial strength varies (independent testing found 43–88% of label in melanotan vials), which is exactly why the test dose comes first.

Melanotan I dosing

Melanotan I runs the same routine at the same doses — 500 mcg to 1 mg daily while loading, then once or twice a week — but slower and gentler: colour builds over weeks rather than days, with far less of the nausea, flushing, and libido effects. No separate ritual, no separate math.

Storage & Handling

🌡️ Temperature

Store in the refrigerator (36°F to 46°F) both before and after reconstitution. Do not freeze a mixed vial.

🌑 Light Exposure

Peptides are sensitive to UV light. Keep vials in their boxes or a dark container.

📅 Shelf Life

Once mixed with BAC water, use the vial within 28 days to ensure sterility and potency.

The original. A full-length copy of the tanning hormone that works almost entirely on the skin's pigment receptor — so the experience is simple: a slow, even tan, and not much else.

The licensed medicine, briefly: as Scenesse, afamelanotide is approved for one rare disease — erythropoietic protoporphyria, where sunlight causes severe skin pain (EU 2014, US 2019). In that form it is a 16 mg implant a clinician places under the skin every two months, and it roughly doubled-to-multiplied patients' pain-free time in the sun in trials. That is the whole of its medical career — everything below is the injected form users actually meet.

As an injection, Melanotan I is bought as a research peptide and used exactly as the How They're Used section describes. What users report is a tan that builds gradually over several weeks of daily dosing, deepening with sun exposure, with mild nausea the only regular complaint — none of Melanotan II's libido surge, appetite crash, or flushing. Two cautions travel with it: it darkens existing moles and freckles (the medicine's label requires a full skin check twice a year), and grey-market vials carry the same purity question as every unregulated peptide — the certificate on a website is a claim, not a measurement you can watch.

The ring. Stronger, cheaper to make, and heard by four receptor systems at once — which is why one injection can tan the skin and stir libido, kill appetite, and bring a wave of nausea.

Those tiny trials prove the effects are real — tanning, erections, desire, appetite loss, yawning. Everything beyond them is case reports and user accounts: there is no safety database and no honest percentage table for illicit use, because nobody counts the users.

What the case reports show

Changing & eruptive moles — and melanoma cases

Moles darkening fast, erupting within days, and reported melanomas. The causal share is debated — users tan heavily too — but MT-II changes moles, which can mask the changes melanoma detection depends on.

Priapism

The trial effect with no off-switch: a prolonged, painful erection needing emergency care.

Rhabdomyolysis

Muscle breakdown after a self-injection (2012) — a kidney-threatening emergency.

Renal infarction

A kidney infarct attributed to MT-II in a 2020 case report.

PRES with seizures

A brain emergency typically driven by a blood-pressure surge, reported in 2013.

The everyday list

Severe nausea and vomiting, flushing, appetite loss, stretching and yawning, spontaneous erections.

The product itself is a second gamble

Australia's regulator found the same chaos in 2026: nasal sprays labelled 30 mg held anywhere from 22 to 54 mg. Two unknowns multiply — what the drug does in you, and what is actually in the bottle.

“Unlicensed, unregulated, and potentially harmful.”
— BMJ, 2009, on melanotan use in the general population

Regulators have said so for years: the FDA warned in 2007 (the seller later pleaded guilty to federal charges); Denmark and the UK warned in 2008; Australia's TGA treats supply in any form as illegal. None of that has stopped the market — it just means the buyer is the entire quality department.

Why the nasal spray can't dose honestly

  • No study exists. No nasal melanotan product has ever had its absorption measured in humans; the percentages on vendor sites have no published study behind them.
  • The nose fights back. Peptides are large and water-loving; the nose offers a small surface, clears material to the throat in about 15–20 minutes, and digests peptides with its own enzymes.
  • The bottle is unknown. No set amount per spray, peptide degrading in solution, and lab findings of 22–54 mg in bottles all labelled 30 mg.

Injection risks

Non-sterile manufacture plus self-injection means contamination, abscess, and wrong-dose risks — with no monitoring and no recall system. Fresh sterile equipment every time is the floor, not a refinement.

Interactions — the honest version

No interaction studies exist for either compound in any form. Warnings about combinations — blood-pressure drugs, erection drugs, appetite suppressants — are reasoning about additive effects, not studied facts.

If you notice any of these side effects with Melanotan II

Regulators' advice, in plain terms: stop — don't finish the vial. Get a full skin check promptly, and treat these as emergencies:

  • 🔎 Any new, changing, or bleeding mole
  • ⏰ An erection lasting over 4 hours
  • 💪 Muscle pain with dark urine
  • 🧠 Severe headache, vision change, or seizure
  • 💧 Passing much less urine

Take the product and its packaging with you.
