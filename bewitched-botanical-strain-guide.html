🔬 The evidence system

How every claim in this guide is graded — and where the record runs out.

🔬

Where the effects come from. Bewitched Botanical publishes almost no speed or effect grading of its own, so every profile here is built from the shop’s own review corpus — roughly 175 customer reviews, read in full for every product — cross-checked against the vendor’s recipe and copy. Where reports and vendor copy conflict, reports win, and the conflict is printed (Horseman’s tag, Enchanted Full Moon’s coffee, Moon Drops’ name). Effect percentages in each strain’s chart are normalized from those report-based ratings — nothing is invented to fill a gap.

The flags are part of the data. Two cards rest on vendor description alone because no community reports exist: 1692 (zero reviews) and Liminal Space (one content-free review). Scream Queen carries an era-split record — a 2024 phenomenon, a far weaker 2025 file — and both eras are printed. A handful of reviewers (one systematic voice above all) author a large share of the corpus, and duplicate reviews exist in it; averages run warm, so this guide weights detailed comparative reports over star counts.

On lab testing: unusually for a small shop, real COAs are published — Wonderland Labs certificates for six base strains under one batch number — but no blend of any kind has a published COA, and neither does the flagship Gold Bali. Full detail in the lab reference.

Stated plainly — the Reddit gap. This vendor’s customer base is visibly Reddit-recruited (one reviewer thanks “reddit people” for the referral), but Reddit itself could not be retrieved with the research tools available, and no Bewitched-specific Reddit threads surfaced in general search. Reddit sentiment is therefore an open gap in this guide — not silence, and not a consensus claimed either way. YouTube coverage exists only at hobbyist scale (Kratom Planet, Kr8tom Chai).

My standing: I am a verified buyer — two orders, eight products, in October 2026 (orders #62190 and #62230). Cards for products in my own orders carry the gold Verified Buyer badge. A third household shipment (order #100908, placed for a friend) is deliberately not used as evidence: its contents are not in my purchase records, so this guide does not state them.

🌱 The shop: one woman, small batches, honest drift

Who is behind the counter, how ordering actually works, and where the website doesn’t keep up with the shelves.

🌱

The operation. Bewitched Botanical LLC is a one-woman shop: Candis, in the Tishomingo area of Oklahoma, blending personally since 2020. “Hi there, I’m Candis! I’m obsessed with all things natural, magical, and a little mysterious.” The catalog runs 43 kratom products (34 powders, 4 capsule products, 2 kilos, 2 sample packs, 1 potentiator) plus a “Mystical Goods” side shop. The community texture is real: blends are collaborations with customers and memorials to them, reviewers write like friends of the house, and orders arrive fast with a free sample tucked in — “Candis is sweet and has the cutest packaging ever.”

How ordering works. Charmingly manual: kilos, split kilos and sample-pack picks are specified in free-text checkout notes (“Tell me what you want in the notes section at checkout please!!!”). That is a genuine error surface — note an out-of-stock pick and it cannot be filled. Exchanges or store credit only, notice within 5 days, product unopened with heat seal intact; payment is due within 24 hours or the order auto-cancels. Shipments to West Virginia (my state) require ID verification. No shipping rate is published anywhere — cost appears at checkout only, and at least one reviewer has complained about it on the record.

Honest drift, itemized. A credible guide names these: the FAQ page is unfinished placeholder Latin, published and never completed. Pink Elf’s prose praises two strains that are not in its own recipe. Red Panji’s copy says “Unavailable” on a product you can order. The banner’s no-ship state list and the Shipping page’s list do not match. Gold Bali’s page still carries an old “18 years old” template block while the rest of the site enforces 21+. None of it is product fraud — it is a single operator’s website trailing her shelves. Practical consequence: treat recipes and stock status as best confirmed at checkout.
