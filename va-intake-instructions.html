Short Guide · 6A Mental Health Unit

Intake Procedures

The admission as a straight workflow — before the patient arrives, on arrival, then the five CPRS notes in the order they run, and the finish.

Before You Start

On off tours (4:30 PM – 8 AM and weekends), rescue meds — PO & IM — must be in place before the patient can enter the unit. Detox admissions: CIWA order with Ativan 1 mg & 2 mg, PO & IM; opioids — COWS with Bentyl, Zofran, Clonidine.

Phase 1Before the patient arrives
1

Consent signed; admission orders in CPRS, including rescue meds; CSRE completed by Social Work or provider; COVID test negative.

2

Check ED vitals and labs. Alcohol admissions: BAL must be 299 or below — above that goes medical, or fluids and a redraw at 299 or below.

3

Note equipment needs: CPAP, prosthetics, walker or wheelchair, fall risk, medical bed.

Phase 2On arrival
4

ED calls — go down, get report from the ED nurse, evaluate the Veteran. The IV comes out. ED staff accompany you and the Veteran up to the unit.

5

Take vitals with height and weight. Issue a BP cuff and write their name + room number on it as you take the initial vitals.

6

Issue the hygiene bag and explain its purpose — cover Oral Care, a required intake education item. Give them a 6A folder and cover the Rules of Behavior.

7

Two 6A staff (at least one RN) do the skin assessment and hazardous-items search — check under underwear and socks; the patient changes clothes and their clothes get washed. Street clothes come up in the white plastic bag, valuables in the manila envelope; extras go to baggage.

8

Have the patient sign the Behavioral Agreement form and turn it in to the unit clerk. Not on the 6A roster? Call the AOD before settling them.

Note 1VAAES Mental Health NSG Admission Screen — open first
9

In CPRS, on the Notes tab: go to New Note, search Admission Screen, and open the “VAAES MENTAL HEALTH NSG ADMISSION SCREEN”. Work it top to bottom — it starts with the Allergy/ADR Review and Medication Review, then General Information through Anticipated Discharge Needs (27 sections).

10

Confirm the C-SSRS was completed within 24 hours prior to admission. Facility transfers: do the Columbia on this note and let Social Work know. Do PHQ-9 / Columbia if due in Clinical Reminders, and complete the COVID reminder.

Note 2Nursing Acute Care Admission Data Base
11

Back to New Note, search Acute Care, and open the “NURSING ACUTE CARE ADMISSION DATABASE, [patient name]” note. Header vitals auto-pull; then arrival mode, handoff, emergency contact and Primary Next of Kin; overall appearance; the main concern in the patient's words — use the chief complaint as the blurb.

12

Work the systems assessment in order: Neuro → EENT → Respiratory → Cardiovascular → Musculoskeletal → GI → GU → Psychosocial → Skin with the Braden Scale → IV sites/infusions. Close the note with Organ Donor and Disposition of Belongings.

Note 3Behavioral Agreement Note
13

Chart the one entry under “Signed Agreement:” — Veteran reviewed the behavioral agreement, denied questions/concerns, verbalized understanding, and signed the agreement (the paper form was signed at Step 8 and filed with the unit clerk).

Note 4Patient Education
14

Chart the note's standard text: tour of the unit given; Welcome to 6A unit packet and Recovery handout given and discussed; patient told to notify staff of any changes, concerns, or questions; universal fall precaution education done; Veteran verbalized good understanding. (Oral Care was covered at Step 6.)

Note 5VAAES Skin Inspection/Assessment
15

Chart from the two-staff inspection (Step 7): skin color, temperature, moisture, turgor; the Braden Skin Assessment — six subscales, total score and risk statement; interventions (needed / not needed); skin integrity. Close with the line: hazardous items found / none found during the skin assessment completed with another RN — cosign.

FinishMental Health Suite, blurb, Kardex
16

Mental Health Suite — Care Plan: select New Plan – Profile and build by section: 1 problem (nursing focused), 1 goal in their own words, 1 objective, and 4 or more nursing interventions, addressed daily.

17

The blurb — switch to Word, print, tape: write the synopsis of the presentation and precipitants in Microsoft Word, print it, and tape it on NOK.

18

Verify orders — and the Kardex: verify the orders (sometimes the charge nurse does this). Then in VISTA Kardex: press E for Edit, then E again — check Admitting DX, Allergies, Diet, Advance Directive, *** and Flags, CIWAs, COWS, finger sticks, anything unusual, 1:1.

What You Get

A complete admission: all five CPRS notes charted in order, Care Plan live in the Mental Health Suite, Behavioral Agreement signed and filed, belongings accounted for, blurb taped, and the Kardex current.
