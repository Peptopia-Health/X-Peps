Key Compounds & Terms

CompoundWhat it is
MitragynineKratom's dominant alkaloid — roughly 60% of the leaf's total alkaloid content and typically 0.5–2% or more of plain leaf by weight. A partial agonist at μ-opioid receptors: stimulant-like at low doses, sedating at higher ones. It is the number most COAs report.
7-Hydroxymitragynine (7-OH)A far more potent μ-opioid agonist than mitragynine. Fresh leaf holds only traces (about 0.01–0.04%) — most 7-OH forms after harvest by oxidation, and in the body when the liver enzyme CYP3A4 converts mitragynine. Noticeably elevated levels are a fingerprint of fortified or extract products.
