Reconstitution Basics

Because these are multi-compound blends, ensuring proper and gentle reconstitution is vital to preserving the delicate bonds of all three (or four) peptides in the vial. Reconstitute using Bacteriostatic Water (BAC):

1

Prep & Sanitize 🧼

Wash hands thoroughly. Pop the plastic caps off both the Peptide blend vial and the BAC water vial. Swab both rubber stoppers with an alcohol prep pad and let them air dry completely.

2

Draw BAC Water 💉

Using a sterile mixing syringe, draw the required amount of Bacteriostatic Water. Ensure there are no large air bubbles in the syringe before injecting.

3

Inject & Dissolve 💧

Pierce the peptide vial. Do not spray the water directly onto the powder. Angle the needle so the water drips slowly down the glass wall of the vial. Direct pressure can easily destroy fragile peptide chains.

4

Swirl Gently 🔄

NEVER SHAKE. Gently roll the vial between your palms or swirl it slowly in circles. Note: Because of the GHK-Cu, the resulting liquid will be a beautiful, vibrant blue color.

Peptopia 30mg Vial Protocol

We specifically formulate GLOW and KLOW in large 30mg research vials. Follow these exact steps for perfect reconstitution and dosing:

  • Draw exactly 3.0mL of Bacteriostatic Water into a large sterile mixing syringe (typically a 3mL luer-lock syringe).
  • Gently inject the 3.0mL of BAC water into the 30mg lyophilized powder using the slow-drip method above.
  • The Math: This 3mL to 30mg ratio creates a concentration of 10mg per 1mL. On a standard U-100 insulin syringe, drawing 10 Units (0.1mL) equals exactly 1,000mcg (1mg) of the peptide blend.

Side Effects & Safety

While generally well-tolerated, the combination of multiple peptides means researchers should be aware of a few potential mild reactions:

  • Injection Site Sensitivity: Because of the Copper in GHK-Cu, injections of GLOW or KLOW can sometimes sting more than standard peptides. Diluting with slightly more BAC water can help alleviate this.
  • Mild Flushing: Some users experience a temporary warmth or flush shortly after administration.
  • Stomach Upset: BPC-157 works heavily on the gut mucosa; mild gastrointestinal shifts can occasionally occur during the first few days of a cycle.
