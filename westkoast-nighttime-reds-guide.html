How to read the bars: scores are community ratings out of 5 from the WestKoast strain guide. For nighttime, you want Calm high and Energy low — the Night score combines exactly those two: Night = (Calm + (5 − Energy)) ÷ 2. WestKoast publishes no Sleep meter, so the Night score is our derivation from those two meters, not a vendor number. The rest of the red shelf runs faster and brighter, for daytime.
