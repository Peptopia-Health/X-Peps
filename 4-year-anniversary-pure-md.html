🌿 The leaf

A pure Maeng Da released for the vendor’s fourth anniversary — batch A34, the “Birthday Batch.” DTE’s listing published no vein and deferred the spec to the tester round — and the round has now reported, making this batch its #1 box. The tester reports below are its spec.

🧪 Lab notes — No public COA for this batch: DTE has published no lab panel for the A34 round — its COA page carries no A34 section, and DTE tests for cleanliness only, so no mitragynine or 7-OH figures exist for this batch. The only lab statement on the bag is the generic line that 7-OH is under Colorado’s limit (2% or less of the alkaloids).

🧪 The lab record — honestly empty

No COA exists for this batch, and this page will not pretend otherwise.

🧪

DTE has published no certificate of analysis for the A34 round — its COA page carries no A34 section as of November 20, 2026, and the vendor’s stated policy is to test for cleanliness only, without publishing alkaloid panels for current drops. There is therefore no mitragynine percentage, no total-alkaloid figure, and no 7-OH number for this batch in any public source. The only lab-related statement attached to the product is the bag’s generic label line that 7-OH is under Colorado’s limit (2% or less of the alkaloids). Everything scored on this page comes from the tester round and the community record — which, for this batch, is unusually complete.
