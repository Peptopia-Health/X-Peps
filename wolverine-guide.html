What Is the Wolverine Blend?
Two repair peptides, one vial — the family's deep-tissue half.

Wolverine is the research-market name for a fixed 1:1 blend of BPC-157 and TB-500 — the two best-known healing peptides, freeze-dried together in a single vial.

A division of labor: BPC-157 raises the repair signal at the injury — new blood vessels, repair cells switched on. TB-500 handles the movement, freeing repair cells to travel into damaged tissue. One calls the crew; the other gets it there.

Built for: tendon & ligament strains · muscle tears · joint aches · post-surgery recovery — slow-healing tissues, where blood supply is the limit.

BPC-157 — the local signal

A synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide modeled on a protective protein in human gastric juice. It switches on VEGFR2 — the receptor that builds new capillaries — and raises growth-hormone receptors in tendon cells.

TB-500 — the migration crew

The active fragment of thymosin beta-4, a protein in nearly every cell. It regulates actin — the scaffolding cells crawl on — so repair cells move into injured tissue fast.

Why they are paired

Tendons and ligaments heal slowly — their blood supply is poor. BPC-157 fixes the supply problem; TB-500 fixes the movement problem. Both bottlenecks, covered.

Dosing: Two Schedules, One Vial
BPC-157 runs daily; TB-500 loads, then maintains. The blend meets both.

The two peptides keep different calendars: BPC-157 is a daily microgram peptide; TB-500 is a milligram peptide, front-loaded twice a week, then maintained weekly. The blend splits the difference — one daily draw carries both.

The one trade, stated plainly.

At 250 mcg of each daily, the blend's TB-500 totals about 1.75 mg a week — maintenance level, not a loading dose. Chasing a full load (4–5 mg a week)? Separate vials make that easy. The blend's strength is simplicity: one vial, one draw, both covered.

Practical notes: subcutaneous — near the injury for local goals, abdominal fat for systemic effect · rotate sites · fresh syringe every injection · cycles run 4–8 weeks (up to 12), then 2–4 weeks off.

Storage & Handling
Cold, dark, and gentle, start to finish.

🧊 Unopened Vial

Freeze-dried powder stays refrigerated, dark, and sealed until mixing day. The water vial lives in the fridge too.

💧 After Mixing

Refrigerate at 2–8 °C; use within about 30 days. Never freeze a mixed vial; discard cloudy or particle-filled solution.

🖐 Handling Rules

Swirl, never shake. Fresh sterile syringe for every draw; rotate sites; label the mixed vial with date and concentration.

Sport
WADA-prohibited at all times — BPC-157 (S0), TB-500 (S2.3)
Prohibited

September 2023. The FDA placed both peptides in Category 2 of its 503A framework — pharmacies could not compound them.

April 2026. The FDA removed both from Category 2. Removal is not approval — neither sits on the approved bulks list.

July 23, 2026. FDA's compounding advisory committee voted 8–6 to recommend 503A listing for both. Advisory only.

October 2026. Compounding reported to be proceeding under FDA enforcement discretion, rulemaking pending.

Reported effects. From the small human reports and clinic use: headache, nausea, dizziness, fatigue, injection-site redness or itching. Two of the three small BPC-157 reports recorded no adverse events.

Evidence, in one line. Most research so far is preclinical — the human record is a few small reports, and the blend has never been through a trial.
