Alkaloid Science & Product Characterization

The consumer record reliably demonstrates that different kratom color phenotypes present distinct physiological effects. While laboratory certificates of analysis (COAs) map the primary alkaloids (mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine), they do not capture the full entourage effect of minor alkaloids and traditional curing methodologies. The exact pharmacological mechanism—the "why" behind the diverse profiles—remains an open scientific question, but the characterizations and effects listed in this guide reflect established, consistent product analysis.
