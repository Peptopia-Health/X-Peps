Vein Color Feedback

Community Reports · Recorded as Fact

The color system is the community's own language: reds for winding down, whites for lift-off, greens for the all-day middle, golds and yellows for mellow warmth. Across years of community reports on this leaf, consumers consistently describe distinct, reproducible effect profiles per color. Those reports are the factual record this guide is built on.

What lab work adds to the picture: First Coast publishes a real testing protocol (heavy metals and microbes, with named AOAC methods) and posts a lab sheet on its product pages — but the sheets are images, and no mitragynine percentage could be read or verified from them. The one hard number in hand is the Green Zareena bag's own printed label: mitragynine 1.5%, 7-hydroxymitragynine 0.01% — a mid-potency plain leaf with a focus-and-mood chemistry, exactly what its scores show. Why leaf presents so differently by color in consumers' hands — minor alkaloids, curing, grind, leaf maturity — remains an open research question. The community's answer is simpler: the colors present differently, reliably, and usefully. This guide treats those consumer reports as its primary evidence and presents them without speculation.

One more thing the community itself repeats: effects vary — often significantly — from one person to the next. Hydration, hours of sleep, other medications, body chemistry, tolerance, and even a recent meal all change how the same leaf from the same harvest presents. Two buyers can split one bag and file two different reports, and both are telling the truth. Read every profile in this guide — this section included — as the center of a range, not a promise.

The Company

Jacksonville, Florida · Family-Owned · The Rebrand in the Open

First Coast Tea Co. is a family-owned company in Jacksonville, Florida — FIRST COAST TEA COMPANY, LLC, on Florida's corporate record since 2019 and active through 2026. Its public storefront sells artisan teas, single-origin coffees, kava, and other botanicals. Its kratom is sold quietly, under the name "speciosa": the word kratom appears nowhere on the site, and the range only appears when the store is searched for that word.

The company is the successor to SoCal Herbal Remedies (SoCal Kratom) of California — the same owners, rebranded and moved across the country in 2019. First Coast does not mention that history anywhere on its site; this guide does, in the reality check, because a review that hides the record is not a review.

What the record shows on the ground is more ordinary: budget prices, a wide strain list, fast dispatch, and courteous service — with a thin public file and a consistency caveat that follows the company from its SoCal days. The eight cards in this guide come from a November 2026 order: one full bag and seven sample packets.

Vendor Reality Check — Claims vs. Verifiable Evidence

What Checks Out · What Does Not · What Cannot Be Checked

The hidden catalog

The word "kratom" appears nowhere on firstcoastteaco.com. The range is labelled "herbal tea," named only "speciosa," and reachable only through a site search for that word. kratom.org calls this what it looks like — a transparency problem — and every major reviewer flags it. The range itself is real, current, and wide; the hiding is a choice, and buyers should know it is one.

The SoCal history

First Coast Tea Co. previously operated in California as SoCal Herbal Remedies. In 2019 a wrongful-death lawsuit was filed against SoCal after a death the coroner attributed to mitragynine; causation was contested in coverage, and the full toxicology was never publicly disclosed. The company later moved operations to Florida under the First Coast name. The company does not mention this history on its site.

AKA GMP — listed, with a live caveat

The company displays the AKA GMP seal on its product pages, the American Kratom Association's own blog listed First Coast among its qualified vendors in 2021, and every reviewer describes it as GMP-listed. When this guide was built, the company's place on the AKA's current qualified-vendor directory could not be confirmed — GMP status renews annually, and "listed historically" is what the evidence supports today. GMP qualification is an audit of manufacturing and labelling practices, not an endorsement of any product.

Lab claims — protocol real, values unverifiable

The Quality Assurance page describes real testing (metals and microbes, named AOAC methods). The product-page lab sheets are images whose values cannot be read or checked, and the independent tracker KratomTest holds zero COAs for this vendor — its 52/100 grade D scores the missing data, not any tested failure. A 2023 peer-reviewed study of GMP vendors' websites scored this site's consumer health information among the weakest in the set. Claim by claim: the protocol checks out; the numbers do not — because they cannot be read.

What no source supports

No source found accuses this company of fraud, non-delivery, or contaminated product. The criticism that the record actually supports is narrower and consistent: hidden products, an undisclosed history, unverifiable lab values, and hit-or-miss consistency.

Buying, Shipping & Policies

Same-Day Dispatch · USPS · Unopened-Only Returns

Finding the products. The kratom range is not linked from the storefront — search the site for "speciosa." Powders come in 1 oz and 250 g bags, plus a Single Strain KG; capsules are a single 60-count product.

Shipping. Orders placed by 2:30 PM EST, Monday–Friday (10 AM Saturday) are promised same-day dispatch by USPS; the one independent order test shipped next day and arrived in six days, and kratom.org puts typical delivery at two to three business days. There is no free-shipping tier — the standing caveat next to this vendor's budget prices.

Customer service. Reviewers consistently describe responsive, courteous service, with replies usually inside the same business day — the bright spot of this vendor's record.

Returns — the weak point. Unopened products only, within 30 days. No refunds on opened product and no satisfaction guarantee; kratom.org lists the returns policy among its cons.
