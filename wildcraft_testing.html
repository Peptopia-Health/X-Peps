How This Test Runs

The rules of this review — five days, three strains, and nothing changed mid-stream.

  • Five days — Monday Nov 2 through Friday Nov 6, 2026.
  • Three sessions a day — one per strain, spaced through the day.
  • Same conditions each session — weighed dose, noted time, ratings filled in while the session is fresh.
  • Rate every effect 0–10 — Energy, Focus, Mood, Calm, Analgesia, Sleep — plus an Overall.
  • The journal saves itself — every entry is kept on this device automatically. Use the buttons under the Scoreboard to print a copy or copy a text summary, or use any day's own Print Day button for a one-day report.

Review Standards

What a reviewer is expected to check and do — the yardsticks this journal is run against.

The lab checks

  • Batch identity first. The batch number on the bag must match the batch on the lab report. Here: Batch #1020 is stamped on all three bags, front and back.
  • Independent, accredited third-party lab — never the vendor's own bench. WildCraft's batches go to Murray Brown Laboratories, and each batch is quarantined until its results are in.
  • Alkaloid panel: mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine, stated as percentages. Typical natural leaf runs about 1.0–1.7% mitragynine; Batch #1020's published line average is ~1.6%.
  • Heavy metals: lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury — measured against Prop 65 / USP limits.
  • Microbial panel: Salmonella and E. coli must be absent; total plate count and yeast & mold within limits.
  • Batch-specific COA, public. A real certificate names the batch, the lab, and the date — and a buyer can read it before ordering. The AKA GMP programme and the state Kratom Consumer Protection Acts both require exactly this.

The review method

  • Weighed doses on a scale, recorded in grams — never scoops by eye. (Day 1's White dose: 2 grams, photographed on the scale.)
  • One variable at a time — same dose routine, same spacing between sessions, same conditions day to day, so differences belong to the strain, not the setup.
  • Notes written during the session and scored within the hour — not reconstructed from memory at the end of the week.
  • The whole record, good and bad. Disappointments and side effects get the same ink as praise.
  • The relationship is disclosed. These samples were provided by WildCraft for review — that fact travels with any verdict sent back.

Standards drawn from the American Kratom Association's GMP Standards Program and the Kratom Consumer Protection Act model, as summarised at thekratom.org's vendor & lab-testing guide and the KCPA explainer.

My Reviews — Batch #1020

The finished write-ups, ready to post — written from this journal and the scores below.

Pure White Vein — Batch #1020 · MIT 1.60%

This is the white I judge other whites against right now. At my standard 2-gram serving it came on clean in about twenty minutes — bright, forward energy with real focus underneath it, and none of the jittery, over-caffeinated edge a strong white can have. It settles into what I can only call autopilot: the work in front of you just gets done.

The bag promises Energy, Focus, Vitality, and it delivers all three in that order. Mood rides up with it and stays level for a solid four-plus hours before tapering off smooth. For a morning or workday white, this is top shelf.

  • ⚡ Energy — strong, clean lift
  • 🎯 Focus — locked in, no scatter ⭐
  • 😊 Mood — bright and steady
  • 🧘 Calm — low; this one is a driver, not a relaxer
  • 🌙 Pain relief — mild background help
  • 💤 Sleep — none; daytime only

⭐ Overall: 9/10 · Dose: 2.0 g weighed · Onset ~20 min · Duration 4+ hrs

— Queeny

Pure Green Vein — Batch #1020 · MIT 1.56%

The standout of the batch for me. This is the most complete green I have had in a long while — energy enough to move, a mood lift you can actually feel, and a calm body underneath it all at the same time. Nothing fights anything else; it just works as one piece.

Mid-spectrum is exactly right: it never pushed me up like a white or pulled me down like a red. I reached for this one more than the other two, at any hour. If the whole #1020 batch is judged by its best bag, this is the bag.

  • ⚡ Energy — moderate, all-day
  • 🎯 Focus — clear and unhurried
  • 😊 Mood — the headline: a strong, feel-good lift ⭐
  • 🧘 Calm — present, body-level ease
  • 🌙 Pain relief — good for a green
  • 💤 Sleep — neutral; fine any time of day

⭐ Overall: 9.5/10 · Dose: 2 g · Onset ~20 min · Duration 4–5 hrs

— Queeny

O.G. Wild Red Vein — Batch #1020 · MIT 1.62%

Full disclosure first: I am not a red drinker. Reds usually knock me flat, so I judge them harder than any white or green. This one earned its score anyway. The relief is real — shoulders down, body quiet — and the relaxation builds slow and deep instead of hitting like a hammer.

At a smaller serving it stayed daytime-friendly and I still got things done; a full serving is an evening strain, no question, and it carried me toward sleep exactly the way a good red should. If you ARE a red drinker, add a point — this is your bag.

  • ⚡ Energy — low, by design
  • 🎯 Focus — soft; not its job
  • 😊 Mood — warm and content
  • 🧘 Calm — deep
  • 🌙 Pain relief — strong; the headline ⭐
  • 💤 Sleep — strong at a full serving

⭐ Overall: 8/10 · Dose: 2 g · Onset ~30 min · Long evening legs

— Queeny

Final Verdict — Batch #1020

Batch #1020 is the most consistent WildCraft batch I have tested. Three strains, three lanes, no weak bag: the Pure Green is the standout — a 9+ in my book, the most complete green I have had in a long while — the Pure White is a clean, focused 9, and the O.G. Red earns an honest 8 from someone who does not gravitate to reds. The labs back the leaf up: 1.56 to 1.62 percent mitragynine across all three. My reorder list starts with the Green and the White.

  • ⚪ White — Pure White Vein: clean, focused energy. 9/10
  • 🟢 Green — Pure Green Vein: the standout; the most complete green in a long while. 9.5/10
  • 🔴 Red — O.G. Wild Red Vein: deep relief and calm, honestly scored by a non-red drinker. 8/10

— Queeny
