Reconstitution Basics

Research blends usually come in single vials containing equal or specific parts of both peptides (e.g., 5mg BPC-157 + 5mg KPV). Reconstitute with Bacteriostatic Water (BAC):

1

Prep & Sanitize 🧼

Wash hands thoroughly. Pop the plastic caps off both the Peptide blend vial and the BAC water vial. Swab both rubber stoppers with an alcohol prep pad and let air dry.

2

Draw BAC Water 💉

Using a sterile syringe, draw the required amount of BAC Water. (Standard Math: Adding 2mL of BAC to a 5mg/5mg vial yields 250mcg of each peptide per 0.1mL/10 units on an insulin syringe).

3

Inject & Dissolve 💧

Pierce the peptide vial. Do not spray directly onto the powder. Angle the needle so the water drips slowly down the glass wall of the vial to protect the fragile peptide bonds.

4

Swirl Gently 🔄

NEVER SHAKE. Gently roll or swirl the vial between your fingers until the solution is completely clear and dissolved. It is now ready for research.

Systemic vs. Localized Administration

Unlike many peptides, BPC-157 has systemic effects regardless of injection site. However, for acute physical trauma (like a torn ligament), many researchers prefer administering the peptide subcutaneously near the site of the injury for maximum localized healing, while relying on its systemic properties to heal the gut concurrently.

Storage & Handling

Temperature

Store in the refrigerator (36°F to 46°F) both before and after reconstitution. Do not freeze.

Light Exposure

Peptides are highly sensitive to UV light. Keep vials in their boxes or a dark container.

Shelf Life

Once mixed with BAC water, discard the vial after 28 days to ensure sterility and efficacy.
