5-Amino 1MQ

A breakthrough NNMT inhibitor for cellular energy, NAD+ salvage, and stimulant-free metabolic acceleration.

What is 5-Amino 1MQ?

Unlike injectable peptides, 5-Amino 1MQ is a small, membrane-permeable molecule. It functions by specifically blocking the enzyme NNMT (Nicotinamide N-methyltransferase). By inhibiting NNMT, it forces the body to salvage and produce more NAD+, which dramatically increases cellular energy (ATP) and directly shrinks fat cells.

Administration & Bioavailability

Because 5-Amino 1MQ is a highly bioavailable small molecule, it does not require reconstitution, bacteriostatic water, or subcutaneous injection. It is administered entirely orally.

1

Form Factor 💊

5-Amino 1MQ is almost exclusively researched in oral capsule form, typically dosed at 50mg per capsule for easy titration and protocol adjustment.

2

Timing & Absorption ⏱️

It can be taken with or without food. However, because it ramps up cellular ATP and energy production, most researchers prefer administering it in the morning or 30-45 minutes pre-workout.

3

Stacking Synergy 🤝

5-Amino 1MQ stacks exceptionally well with NAD+ precursors (like NMN or NR) to hyper-drive energy, or alongside GLP-1 agonists (like Tirzepatide) to prevent muscle loss while losing weight.

Stimulant-Free Notice

Despite causing significant increases in metabolic rate and energy output, 5-Amino 1MQ does not stimulate the central nervous system (CNS). It will not cause heart palpitations, jitters, or anxiety like caffeine or traditional fat burners.

Storage & Handling

Temperature

Unlike lyophilized peptides, 5-Amino capsules are highly stable at room temperature. Do not refrigerate.

Moisture

Keep capsules in their original sealed container with desiccant packets to prevent moisture degradation.

Light Exposure

Store in a cool, dark place (like a medicine cabinet or drawer) away from direct UV sunlight.
