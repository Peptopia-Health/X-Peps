Storage & Handling

Best practices for preserving the integrity, stability, and efficacy of your research compounds.

The Basics of Stability

Peptides are fragile chains of amino acids held together by delicate bonds. Because of this, their environmental stability changes drastically depending on whether they are in their raw, dry state or mixed with a liquid solution.

Lyophilized (Dry)

❄️ Freeze-dried powder
🛡️ Highly durable & stable
🌡️ Survives room temperature

Reconstituted (Liquid)

💧 Mixed with BAC water
⚠️ Highly vulnerable to degradation
🥶 Requires immediate refrigeration

Nootropics / Orals

💊 Small molecule capsules
📦 Keep sealed with desiccants
🌡️ Room temperature only

Environmental Hazards

To prevent your research materials from rapidly degrading, you must actively protect them from three primary environmental enemies:

Ultraviolet Light ☀️

Prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or harsh UV rays will break down the amino acid sequences inside the vial. Always store your vials in a dark place, such as a closed drawer, a medicine cabinet, or the back of a refrigerator.

Physical Agitation 🫨

Once a peptide has been reconstituted into liquid, the molecular chains become highly susceptible to kinetic damage. Never shake a peptide vial. If you need to mix it, gently roll it between your palms or swirl it slowly.

Heat & Temperature Swings 🔥

Repeatedly moving a vial from a cold fridge to a warm room can introduce condensation and temperature shock. Only remove the vial from the refrigerator when actively preparing for research, and return it immediately afterward.

Quick Reference Storage Table

The 28-Day Rule

Bacteriostatic (BAC) Water contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol, which prevents bacterial growth. However, this alcohol begins to degrade the peptide itself over time. For maximum efficacy and safety, always discard any reconstituted peptide after 28 to 30 days.

Material State Storage Environment Expected Shelf Life Notes
Lyophilized Peptides Deep Freezer (-20°C) 2 - 3 Years Optimal for long-term bulk storage. Allow to reach room temp before mixing.
Lyophilized Peptides Refrigerator (2°C - 8°C) 12 - 18 Months Standard storage protocol for unused powders before reconstitution.
Lyophilized Peptides Room Temp (20°C - 24°C) 3 - 4 Weeks Perfectly safe for transit and shipping; move to fridge upon arrival.
Reconstituted Peptides Refrigerator (2°C - 8°C) 28 Days Mandatory. Liquid peptides must be kept cold to maintain stability.
Reconstituted Peptides Room Temp (20°C - 24°C) Rapid Degradation Leaving mixed vials out will destroy the peptide within 24-48 hours.
Oral Capsules (5-Amino) Room Temp (Dark/Dry) 2 Years Do not refrigerate. Keep sealed with silica desiccant packets.
