How to read the database

🟢 Confirmed
The strain, batch, and exact COA relationship is directly supported by the source material.
🟡 Group confirmed
The batch and/or vein is supported, but the exact internal COA sample is not established.
⚪ Unknown
Do not use an individual COA number for that retail strain without additional documentation.
Why are there two names?
Retail names such as “Green Bumblebee” and lab IDs such as “FC#B.07Green” are separate identifiers. This site keeps them separate unless a source links them.
