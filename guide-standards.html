How Our Guides Are Built

One skeleton, family skins — every guide shares the same bones; only the colors, content rules, and delivery route change.

The Universal Skeleton — Every Guide, No Exceptions

  • Standalone page — no site header or nav inside a guide
  • Top-right cluster — date pill · theme · print, small matched buttons
  • One-line intro — a single concise sentence under the title, never a paragraph
  • Collapsed index — title + icon; the icon opens it; print shows all
  • Slim title bands — centered Title-Case titles, one-line subtitles, minimal height
  • Bands hug their titles — fit-content width, centered; never wider than the widest band
  • Icons are buttons — one unique gloss push-button per band, at the right; the whole band opens its section
  • Sections start collapsed — an open section's band changes color to show it's in use
  • Light theme by default — dark is a per-visit toggle, never remembered
  • Visuals carry weight — diagrams, stat cards, comparison graphics; never bare prose
  • Zero dead links — every pill, bubble, and anchor is audited before publishing
  • Print standard — Letter, exact colors, nothing slices across a page break
  • Fonts — Outfit headings, DM Sans body; never a serif, anywhere
  • One-line footer — the family's credit line, nothing more
  • Restyles preserve content — structure changes; the substance never gets dropped
  • Verified live — opened, clicked, and measured in a real browser before it's called done

The Families — Where the Lines Are Drawn

Kratom Strain Guides

The Vendor's Own Colors

  • Each vendor keeps its own palette (WildCraft forest green, WestKoast greens, TreeLeaf, DTE…)
  • Quick Pick wheel built only from that vendor's strains; pie popups from the guide's own ratings
  • Experience-led: community reports over official positions; no warning or caution statements, ever
  • Footer: “Complete Strain Guide for [Vendor] · Independent Customer Research”
  • Home: Kratom Reviews hub · Drive: Kratom Strain Guides
Nursing Education

Always Purple

  • One purple identity for the whole family — guides and their cards (Learning section + Health Psychology)
  • Clinical and sourced: every number traced to a source actually read; contested points hedged in the text
  • Shape: normals first → level-by-level deep dive → mirror image → emergencies → treatment → nursing assessment
  • Footer: “[Topic] — A Nursing Guide · Independent Nursing Education”
  • The TSH guide is the permanent exemplar for this family
VA Unit Guides

Print-First Workflow

  • Orange headline family; built to be followed at the desk, step by step
  • Exact CPRS note names in the true order (Intake: Screen → Data Base → Agreement → Education → Skin → Suite)
  • Full guide, 2-page Short, 1-page Mini — same workflow, three depths
  • Delivery is gated: chat approval → Drive 🏥 VA Guides → second approval → VA email
  • Never carries sample patient data — field names and order only
Peptopia Education · GLP

Cyan Research Reports

  • Cyan accent family on the Education index (Cagrilintide, Eloralintide…)
  • Trial data leads: named studies, exact percentages, comparison bars with the promoted compound bold green
  • News articles follow the article bones: icon before headline, date bubble, slim hero, graph before Sources, no disclaimer text
  • Weekly GLP update lands on Biohacking News, not the index
Simple Guides

One Page, His Steps

  • Triggered by “Simple Guide: [topic]” — a distilled instruction set, not a production
  • Numbered step cards; at most one before-you-start and one what-you-get note
  • No research phase, no popups, no charts, no sources
  • A step that switches apps says so in its heading
Tuscarora Artifacts

Story-First Doctrine

  • Awning-orange display family on its own portal
  • One fascinating story per artifact — maker, era, culture woven in plain language
  • Findspots in general terms only; no owner's name, no version talk
  • Clickable timeline in every guide; audio capped at two minutes, fireside style
