🌿 What "String-Dried" and "D10" Mean

String-dried is DTE's curing process: fresh kratom leaf is tied into bundles and hung on strings in dark, humidity-monitored indoor rooms before it ever becomes powder. The stated purpose is avoiding thermal and UV degradation to preserve delicate alkaloids. The community-credited result is longer effect duration — the "legs" this strain is known for.

D10 is DTE's farmer-and-batch code. Farmer D is the string-dried line (Farmer A handles old-growth wild harvests, the J-series comes from a riverbank region). D10 is the tenth batch from the D line. D11 is the newer batch — the current DTE menu lists "OG Imperial Green Dragon D11," so a D10 bag is one generation behind. Same strain name, potentially different character. That's the honest caveat baked into DTE's small-batch model.

Imperial Green Dragon sits in DTE's Green Dragon family alongside Wild Green Dragon (A27), Super Green Dragon (A31), 20-Year Wild Green Dragon (K20), and Ancient Green Dragon (sun-dried, SD1). The Imperial variant is the string-dried premium tier of that family.

👎 Honest Downsides

Availability

Strains sell out regularly. The no-limits window is brief, then one-bag-per-size limits kick in. Stock-up buyers get frustrated — and a D10 bag is already superseded by D11.

Price premium

$115/kg vs. budget competitors around $90/kg. You're paying for small-batch curation and included shipping.

Batch variance

Same name doesn't mean same leaf. D10 vs. D11 can differ in spec. DTE is transparent about this — specs get revised from tester data — but reviews of one batch don't fully transfer to the next.

Thin independent verification

No Reddit footprint for DTE specifically; discussion concentrates in their own Discord and email list, which is inherently self-selecting. No D10-specific COA located. The "string-drying preserves alkaloids" mechanism is plausible but unproven by independent analysis — the longer legs are reported, the mechanism is the vendor's story.

Strict returns

80%+ remaining, 5 days — effectively no real recourse if a batch doesn't agree with you.
