🔬 Vein colors & what consumers report

What the community's experience shows — recorded here as fact, batch by batch.

🔬

The color system is the community's own language: reds for winding down, whites for lift-off, greens for the all-day middle, golds and yellows for mellow warmth. Across years of reports — on-site reviews, Reddit threads, YouTube reviews, and buyer discussions — consumers consistently describe distinct, reproducible effect profiles per color. Those reports are the factual record this guide is built on, and they show the colors matter in practice.

What lab work adds to the picture: published alkaloid panels (including WestKoast's own COAs in the lab reference, tested by MBL via HPLC-UV) show the major alkaloids clustering in familiar ranges by vein — reds around 1.3–1.5% mitragynine, greens 1.2–1.9%, whites 1.4–2.0% in the July 2026 batches. That is a measured finding about major-alkaloid percentages, not a verdict on the colors themselves. Why leaf presents so differently by color in consumers' hands — minor alkaloids, curing and fermentation, grind, leaf maturity — remains an open research question. The community's answer, recorded across thousands of reports, is simpler: the colors present differently, reliably, and usefully. This guide treats those consumer reports as its primary evidence and presents them without speculation.

🔴 Red strains — deep calm & evening ease

The wind-down color: deep relaxation & relief, calming body effects, and mental calm for restful rest.

🌙
Reds are the evening shift. WestKoast's red menu leans heavily on two grind lines — UF (ultra-fine) and Nano (nano-grind) — plus a small set of house blends. The standout of the entire red catalog, by a wide margin of evidence, is the house custom blend Red Fuzzy Blanket: fifteen five-star on-site reviews and independent editorial coverage, all pointing at the same thing — night-time comfort. Most other reds carry on-site star ratings with little or no written detail, which this guide marks honestly.
🏆 Red standouts — deepest evidence: Red Fuzzy Blanket 0926 (15× ⭐ 5.0, sleep & pain); heavy ferment: Red Bent 0726 ("not too sedating"); blend base: Red Magic 0926 (unfermented, the Blanket's foundation).

How to read a WestKoast bag.

🌱

WestKoast is a small, one-woman operation (Salem, OR). The labeling system is refreshingly literal — once you know the code, you know exactly what you're holding:

CodeMeaning
MMYY (0526, 0626, 0726, 0926, 1026)Harvest/batch date. 0926 = September 2026 batch. Same strain name + new code = new lot — treat it as a new leaf.
UF (Red Thai UF, Red Malay UF…)Ultra-fine grind line.
Nano (Nano Green Devil, Nano Red Borneo…)Nano-grind line — finer than ultra-fine; the house signature grind.
FC#AR / FC#B / FC#T / FC#DFarmer codes used on COAs (AR, B, T, D sourcing lines). COAs are published per farmer-code batch, not per retail strain name.
Custom Blends (Red Fuzzy Blanket, Pain Blend)House recipes — e.g., Pain Blend = Farmer R red bent + yellow boyan.
The honest way to use WestKoast's COAs: they tell you what July 2026 red-vein leaf from farmer AR tested at — a vein-level batch fingerprint. They cannot tell you what "Nano Red Devil 0926" tests at, because the reports are never mapped to retail names. This guide cites them only at the level they were published.
