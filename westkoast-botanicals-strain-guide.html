Reading a strain card: each card opens with the strain name and its harvest batch code — WestKoast codes are dates (0726 = July 2026 harvest). Six effect chips give the one-glance profile — 🧡 Mood, ⚡ Energy, 🎯 Focus, 😌 Calm, 💚 Pain, 😴 Sleep — and the legs line beneath them tracks how long each strain runs (⏳). Scores (0.0–5.0) reflect community consensus gathered across on-site reviews, Reddit, YouTube, and third-party writeups: 4.5+ elite · 3.5–4.4 strong · 2.0–3.4 moderate · 0.5–1.9 subtle · — = not enough data. Evidence strength is graded on every card — nothing is invented. Where a public lab report exists, the alkaloid table shows the measured numbers.
Effect profiles & the spectrum map: tap the little chart on any strain card for its full effect profile. The chips and profiles chart the five effects this guide scores on every card — Energy, Mood, Calm, Analgesia and Sleep. Focus rides along on the chips at 0.0: this guide's sources describe it (Green Lightning is the catalog's focus green) but never score it, so no number is invented for it. Sleep (the black slice) is a first-class effect here: it has its own lane on the spectrum map below, and a strain's Sleep value is derived from its Calm and Analgesia scores — Sleep = (Calm + Analgesia) ÷ 2, rounded — the same derived value in the profiles, on the map, and on the Kratom Picker's score bars. Spectrum map: every dot jumps to its strain's card and opens it; click the card itself to glide back to the map. One strain, Elite Red, carries no ratings, so it has no chart and no dots — nothing is invented for it.

Vein Colors & What Consumers Report

The Community's Experience, Recorded as Fact · Batch by Batch

The color system is the community's own language: reds for winding down, whites for lift-off, greens for the all-day middle, golds and yellows for mellow warmth. Across years of reports — on-site reviews, Reddit threads, YouTube reviews, and buyer discussions — consumers consistently describe distinct, reproducible effect profiles per color. Those reports are the factual record this guide is built on, and they show the colors matter in practice.

What lab work adds to the picture: published alkaloid panels (including WestKoast's own COAs in the lab reference, tested by MBL via HPLC-UV) show the major alkaloids clustering in familiar ranges by vein — reds around 1.3–1.5% mitragynine, greens 1.2–1.9%, whites 1.4–2.0% in the July 2026 batches. That is a measured finding about major-alkaloid percentages, not a verdict on the colors themselves. Why leaf presents so differently by color in consumers' hands — minor alkaloids, curing and fermentation, grind, leaf maturity — remains an open research question. The community's answer, recorded across thousands of reports, is simpler: the colors present differently, reliably, and usefully. This guide treats those consumer reports as its primary evidence and presents them without speculation.

The Wind-Down Color · Deep Relaxation & Relief, Calming Body Effects & Mental Calm for Restful Rest

Reds are the evening shift. WestKoast's red menu leans heavily on two grind lines — UF (ultra-fine) and Nano (nano-grind) — plus a small set of house blends. The standout of the entire red catalog, by a wide margin of evidence, is the house custom blend Red Fuzzy Blanket: fifteen five-star on-site reviews and independent editorial coverage, all pointing at the same thing — night-time comfort.
